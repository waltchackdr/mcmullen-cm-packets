\begin{abstract}
This supplement records the arithmetic domain, orientation and
module calculations, retained geometric exclusion batches, auxiliary
lattice constructions and execution provenance for two papers on a fixed
CM packet and a finite quotient family. It separates mathematical
exhaustiveness, exact decision procedures and historical execution
assumptions. A subsidiary comparison explains the normalization and
Hecke-search issues relevant to prior work. The document provides an
guide to the retained research evidence; it does not claim a
new full-suite replay or a complete public evidence archive.
\end{abstract}
