\begin{abstract}
We present explicit algebraic-cycle and character-lattice arguments
for products of CM abelian varieties attached to the fixed field
$\mathbb Q(\zeta_{42})$. Integral relation generation, algebraic source
tensors and contractions yield the stated mixed-product algebraicity
results for its eight rank-one sixfold type orbits. The note records the
proof inputs used by the packet and quotient papers and distinguishes
rational Hodge conclusions from integral polarizations and curve
placement. It is a fixed-field supporting calculation, not a claim of a
new general CM Hodge theorem or a verification of a proposed general one.
\end{abstract}
