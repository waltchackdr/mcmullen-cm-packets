\paragraph{Whole-packet execution provenance and regression scope.}
The original-target cover is $116+249=365$ distinct nonvertex points.
Its legacy component is assembled from $150$ retained original commands,
$63$ individually accepted single-mask replacements and two complete
later replacements. The $65$ replacement commands partition nine old
command domains. This is a reviewed equivalent cover, not a claim that
the interrupted old $159$-command pool or the old $65$-job recovery pool
completed normally. Failed and interrupted histories remain retained.
The original-target portable aggregate is bound by
\path{experiments/continuous_v82_review/v82_outcome_review.json}.

Four historical V42/V43 companion commands have no recorded per-command
end-source guard; their use assumes the archived sources and runtime
were not transiently replaced during execution. The original legacy
pool also lacks a common final source/runtime guard. Present hashes
cannot establish the absence of a transient replacement, and no missing
guard is reconstructed. The previously scoped maximal-only acceptance
remains recorded in
\path{experiments/continuous_v82_review/om13_q26_scoped_outcome_review.json}.

The reflected aggregate selects exactly $730$ full backend results:
$557$ original results and $173$ selected continuation results. It
reconciles all $365$ paired empty outcomes, complete row journals and
additional completed duplicates, while retaining failed predecessors.
Its actual outcome and retention are bound by
\path{experiments/continuous_v82_review/v93_actual_conditional730_outcome_retention_review.json}.
One continuation cohort halted after $78$ complete results, with
$26$ interrupted and $69$ unstarted tasks. Its selected $26$ results
and $52$ completed duplicates retain their individual driver checks;
the missing coordinator-wide final guards are not supplied by later
hashes. Use of this cohort retains the explicitly reviewed ordinary
historical source/runtime immutability assumption.

The portable mathematical closure has sixteen files: fourteen unchanged
files and two drivers with reversible root/helper-path relocation.
The nineteen-file executed closure includes its operational wrappers.
Whole-executable comparison covers dynamic imports, active target
installation, plans, globals and actual interpreter/package profiles.
The old expected-data module imported by a retained foundation remains
an inert lineage dependency for these searches: its historical
assertions may abort execution, but it supplies no new-label bounds or
candidate decisions. New comparison expectations are checked only after
complete enumeration.

The portable controls comprise the original sixteen negative labels
plus the exact additional \texttt{R127:pic1:03} pair, hence $34$ full
negative runs, and twelve strict positive runs on the final common
$371$-record package. The added pair exercises the otherwise uncovered
identity-denominator scaling branch. The original $32$-run conjunction
failed that observed-interface requirement and remains a failed record;
it is not renamed as a pass. The revised conjunction retains every
original result comparison and requires coverage of the unchanged
observed interface inventory. Controls test relocation and entrypoint
integration; they do not replace exhaustive results for the other labels.

executions, and the source correspondence is

This is the final packet receipt; the intermediate outcome receipts above

The inventory is exactly $159+504+12+730=1405$ commands. Within the

The existing $159$ command vectors and default four-worker limit are

An immutable source/evidence release, its final manuscript compilation
and visual PDF inspection remain separately recorded verification steps.

The audited local environment uses Python~3.14.6, SymPy~1.14.0, and
Node.js~22.19.0.  The file \path{requirements.txt} pins the SymPy version.
The Sturm implementations written with the Python standard library do not
import SymPy.  The verifier
\path{scripts/run_certificates.py} fails if any registered source is missing,
if a registered program exits nonzero, or if the required Node runtime is
unavailable.  The wrapper \path{verify_all.sh} also checks repository
invariants, compiles the manuscript when a LaTeX engine is available, writes
the full run log, and emits a SHA-256 manifest.  Separate implementations are
machine-verification paths, not independent human proofs.

All theorem-relevant embedding labels in the SymPy certificates are ordered
by exact rational endpoints of pairwise disjoint root intervals, with strict
separation asserted.  Decimal formatting in the independent quadrilateral
module is display-only.  The exact packet verifiers exhaust the CM-character
subsets as a regression check; Lemma~\externalref{packet}{lem:primitivebinary} supplies the
conceptual classification for the primitive block.


\paragraph{Availability of computational evidence.}
The manuscript and research repository are currently private working
materials. The historical receipts and source paths in this supplement refer
to that retained workspace. A complete, versioned package sufficient to
inspect every theorem-relevant dependency has not been released. The
selected source snapshot and its short smoke checks are not such a package.
No assertion of public data availability or fresh full-suite reproduction
is made. Before external circulation, the evidence required by each
computer-assisted result must be assembled and its access instructions
checked against the exact manuscript version.
