\section{Scope and further questions}
The field is fixed throughout. No additional CM point on a curve is
constructed, and isogeny invariance does not identify principal
polarizations. The useful continuation is a precise comparison of the
explicit source cycles and saturated relation presentations with the
classical Fermat results and broader CM constructions. A future general
statement would require its own algebraic inputs and integral generation
proof, rather than extrapolation from this finite character census.
