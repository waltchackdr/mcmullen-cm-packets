# Scope and limitations

## Mathematical and execution assumptions

The three-point packet conclusion retains the explicit historical execution
assumptions stated in Paper I and the supplement. This edition does not claim
a fresh complete certificate replay, complete runtime/import closure or
reconstruction of missing temporal execution evidence.

The standard trace polarization is not principal. Modified principal models
do not establish literal membership in the differently normalized principally
polarized locus. Positive determinant, arbitrary determinant and determinant-one
equivalence remain distinct, as do geometric and companion CM signatures.

The 156 smaller-order Jacobian cases remain open. Partial theta data do not
complete their classification. Remaining definition-degree questions are stated
in Paper II. No general Hodge theorem or theorem about all sixfolds is claimed.

## Review status

Internal checks included model-assisted argument review. They are not external
human specialist approval or a proof audit of every statement. The scoped
individual-powers review does not itself certify the later mixed-eight-type
statement. Novelty and significance remain matters for specialist assessment;
the [prior-work comparison](prior-work.md) does not establish priority.

## Distribution boundary

This edition includes selected mathematical source and evidence. Private
working records, correspondence, local settings, infrastructure details and
unreviewed third-party captures are excluded. Their original identities remain
in private records; omitted objects are not silently replaced or counted as
passing checks. See the [evidence selection](../evidence/README.md).

The edition is a public research-preprint package. Original code/data and
writing now have explicit [license grants](../LICENSE.md). The scoped
[third-party inspection](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) identified no unresolved
bundled-source licensing issue; it is not a legal opinion or proof of ownership.
External specialist assessment remains outstanding. Public availability does
not imply peer review or specialist endorsement.
