## Novelty questions

Compare the precise statements, not just terminology or dates. The proposed
contributions are the specified marked packet geometry, integral principal
family and finite quotient/field classification. Check whether these statements
already follow from, or appear explicitly in, earlier work.

The [prior-work comparison](prior-work.md) identifies classical CM/descent
inputs, the known same-field Jacobians and Goodson's Fermat-type antecedent.
OpenAI's cited general CM Hodge assertion, if valid, subsumes our algebraicity
conclusions. No new general Hodge theorem, general contraction method or
chronological priority claim is being requested. Identify the strongest
defensible contribution for each main paper and any required narrowing.

## Requested response

For each question you assess, please provide:

1. The exact theorem, hypothesis or paragraph considered.
2. A disposition: sound within stated scope, repair needed, not assessed, or
   requires further computation.
3. Any missing implication or counterexample, with the weakest sufficient repair.
4. Any overlapping prior theorem, with a precise citation and explanation.
5. Whether a proposed computation is required for validity or reproducibility.

Please state relevant conflicts and the scope of your review. A short initial
assessment is useful; accepting this brief does not imply reviewing all four
documents. Open questions, including the 156 smaller-order Jacobian cases,
may remain explicitly open at the papers' endpoint.
