# Prior work and proposed contributions

The proposed contribution is an explicit geometric and integral classification
for the specified packet and quotient families. General CM existence,
trace-dual lattices, unit-norm methods and classical descent are prior inputs.

| Topic | Position of this package |
| --- | --- |
| Marked packet on McMullen's curve | Specified arithmetic population, simultaneous geometric exclusion and identification of surviving fibers, under stated execution premises |
| Principal models | Explicit common nonfree subgroup, stabilizer, connected family and integral maps |
| Quotients and polarizations | Classifications with kernel, unpolarized and polarized equivalence relations kept separate |
| Fields and descent | Specified scalar representation, field obstruction over bases containing the stated CM field, and simultaneous construction |
| Hodge algebraicity | Supporting fixed-field arguments and source data; no new general Hodge theorem or general method is asserted |

## Related CM Hodge work

OpenAI's cited manuscript asserts rational Hodge algebraicity for all complex
CM abelian varieties. If correct, that assertion subsumes the algebraicity
conclusions here. Balanced tensors, relations, auxiliary varieties and
polarization contractions overlap with ingredients of the supporting arguments.
A fixed-field implementation or contraction alone is not a new-method claim.
The marked packet positions, integral lattices and specified model fields are
additional data that do not follow from cycle-class surjectivity alone.
See the [cited primary introduction](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/preprints/The-rational-Hodge-conjecture-for-CM-abelian-varieties-October-6-2026/build/sections/01-introduction.tex).

## Same-field and classical antecedents

Goodson's section 5.3 identifies the sixfold CM factor and its Fermat provenance;
these are not discoveries of this package. Li–Mantovan–Pries provide the known
same-field Jacobian and principal-polarization/PEL inputs. Their exact locators
and hypotheses are cited in the papers. The retained Saller comparison also
identifies antecedents for the B/C rational CM-type orbit and balanced quadratic
structure. The integral models and packet positions require further arguments.

The descent discussion credits classical effective descent of suitable marked
polarized CM triples. It does not assert a unique smallest field over arbitrary
bases or descent of a selected ample line bundle where only polarization
morphism descent is proved.

This is a scoped comparison, not an exhaustive literature search or proof of
firstness. The papers' bibliographies identify the sources and versions used.
