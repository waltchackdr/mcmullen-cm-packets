# Research overview

The project studies a specified marked norm packet on McMullen's normalized
Hilbert modular curve and the integral models, polarizations and quotient
families associated with its CM sixfolds.

## The two main questions

**Paper I:** Which points in the fixed packet lie on the curve, and what
principally polarized models arise from those fibers? The retained arithmetic
population has 368 quotient points. The three-point geometric conclusion is
conditional on the manuscript's explicit historical execution premises.
The common self-dual lattice and its connected family are distinguished from
the original, nonprincipal trace-polarized lattice.

**Paper II:** How do the specified finite quotients differ as kernels, underlying
abelian varieties and polarized varieties, and over which fields can the stated
families be defined? These are different equivalence relations. Its descent
claims retain their base-field and actual torsion-action hypotheses.

The supporting Hodge note supplies source cycles and character-relation
arguments. It does not claim the general Hodge conjecture or a new general
method. The supplement contains detailed arithmetic, finite-domain arguments,
execution qualifications and prior-work comparisons.

## Contribution and overlap

The proposed contributions are concrete marked geometric and integral
classifications and the specified simultaneous quotient/descent construction.
General CM existence, known same-field Jacobians and classical polarization
methods are prior inputs. OpenAI's broader CM Hodge assertion, if correct,
subsumes the Hodge-algebraicity conclusions; it does not by itself determine the
marked packet locations, integral lattices or particular model fields.

See the [prior-work comparison](prior-work.md).
It is a scoped literature comparison, not proof of priority or specialist
acceptance. The [limitations](limitations.md) describe the scope of internal checks and outstanding external review.

## Open questions

The papers conclude with explicit research questions. The 156
smaller-order Jacobian cases and the sharp definition-degree questions listed
in the manuscripts are open extensions, not completed results. Literal
identification with Mostaed's differently normalized principally polarized
locus is not asserted. See [limitations](limitations.md) before interpreting
counts or model statements more broadly.
