# Third-party material and dependencies

The source distribution contains project implementations, computational
records, citations and source-identification metadata. The release inspection
identified no bundled external paper, third-party software distribution, or
copied third-party implementation. This is a scoped source inspection, not a
legal opinion or a guarantee of authorship for every expression.

## Fonts embedded in the papers

The four PDFs contain subsets of Latin Modern fonts and AMS mathematical
fonts (MSBM and Euler Fraktur). Those font programs retain their own licenses;
the project's CC BY license covers its writing, not the font software.

- Latin Modern: Bogusław Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki; [GUST Font License](LICENSES/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt), based on [LPPL 1.3c](LICENSES/LPPL-1.3c.txt). See the [TeX Users Group license page](https://www.tug.org/fonts/licenses/gfl.html) for the relationship between these terms.
- AMS fonts: copyright and reserved font names are retained in [the AMS Open Font License notice](LICENSES/AMS-OFL.txt). See [the AMS font distribution](https://ctan.org/pkg/amsfonts). The license permits document embedding and does not require the resulting document to use the font license.

No standalone font files or TeX package distributions are included.

## Software used separately

Python, SymPy and Tectonic are installed separately and are not distributed
here. Historical records may identify other tools, downloads, filenames or
license hashes; these are metadata, not copies of those dependencies. Their
authors' terms apply if those tools are obtained separately. Consult each
tool's own distribution before redistributing it.

related work is in [prior work](docs/prior-work.md). External source URLs and
digests identify material inspected during research; they grant no right to
redistribute the referenced work. Original third-party articles, webpage
captures and repository snapshots are outside this edition.
